## Patterns
- **Articles:** all 214 A1 nouns have the right de/het. No changes.
- **Verbs:** every verb prompt is an infinitive except `kunnen = -ebilmek` (a suffix). Suggested: `yapabilmek`.
- **Meaning:** two prompts use a rarer or harsher sense than A1 needs: `pakken = kapmak` (snatch) and
  `krijgen = elde etmek` (obtain, formal).
- **Same Dutch word twice:** `eten` (verb and noun), `leven` (verb and noun) and `arm` (adjective A1, noun A2). The prompts differ,
  but US-002 forbids two prompts with the same Dutch word in a level. The developer should confirm the level builder compares
  lemmas, not ids; the data can stay.
- **Near-duplicate prompts:** `leggen = yatırmak, koymak` and `zetten = koymak` are in the same category. Other shared head words
  already carry a sense hint (`doen = yapmak` / `maken = yapmak (üretmek)`, `houden = tutmak` / `kosten = (fiyatı) tutmak`).
- **Lemma spelling:** `engels`, `nederlands` should be capitalised in Dutch; the recap shows the lemma as stored.
- **Part of speech:** `vaak`, `rechts`, `links` are adverbs stored as adjectives; harmless, since the schema has no adverb.
- **Categories:** `kam`, `kammen` sit in Giyim; body care fits better. Everything else fits.
- **Sensitive words:** none offensive. Words a learner may notice: `bier` (food), `dik = şişman`, `luis = bit`, `kerk`; all
  are everyday NT2 vocabulary, so no flag.
