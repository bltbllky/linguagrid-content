## Patterns
- **False friends (new pattern, 3 pairs):** a Turkish prompt that is spelled like another Dutch word of the same levels.
  The level builder checks that prompts are unique (ADR-008), not that a prompt differs from the Dutch words in the grid.
  - tak "dal" / extras dal (both A2, a2-nature-weather): row filed;
  - batterij "pil" / extras pil (both A2, a2-home and a2-health): row filed;
  - live honing "bal" (A2, reviewed) / extras bal "top": no row, because the fix would touch a reviewed live word and
    the extras prompt is right. For the owner to decide with the two rows above.
- **Level, A1 vs A2:** long and bot are organ/skeleton words, A2 rather than A1. zeventien and negentien are A2 only
  because their rank is above 3,000 (E7), while every other teen is A1.
- **Level, too hard (4 rows):** drempel, sluipen, overlast, symptoom. Other formal-looking words (verstandig,
  betrouwbaar, uitstellen, mislukken, lening, aankoop, weduwe, opereren) are common in A2 course texts and stay.
- **Prompt pairs (E10):** no extras prompt equals a live, staged B1 or other extras prompt. 40 base words are shared
  (for example "ses" stem/geluid, "sıra" rij/beurt and B1 volgorde, "toplamak" verzamelen and three B1 verbs), each kept
  apart by a hint. getrouwd "evlenmiş" avoids B1 gehuwd "evli" but is not the everyday adjective: row filed.
- **Text:** the remaining choices made to avoid collisions were kept: taak "yapılacak iş" (A2 opdracht has "görev"),
  uitstekend "çok iyi" (A1 perfect has "mükemmel"), letten "özen göstermek" (B1 opletten has "dikkat etmek"),
  tiener "yeniyetme" (B1 puber has "ergen").
- **Category:** stap sits in a2-daily-verbs ("Günlük fiiller"); the live list has 5 nouns there, so no row.
- **Lemma rule, length:** every lemma is a current lemma of ≤ 12 letters; no diminutive or plural-only noun.
