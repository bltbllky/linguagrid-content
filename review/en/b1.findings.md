## Mechanical style checks (all 1,200 rows)
- No noun starts with "the", "a" or "an"; every verb, and only the verbs, starts with "to" (282 verbs).
- British spelling throughout (analyse, organiser, labour, mould, tumour, diarrhoea, catalogue, colourful, criticise,
  sceptical, practise); no American spelling found.
- Capitals follow English rules and match the live file ("English", "Christmas", "WC"): "T-shirt", "DIY store",
  "Friday evening", "Sunday evening", "Sunday afternoon". No row.
- `npm run validate -- --stage b1` shows no shared prompt for a B1 id.

## Patterns
1. **The sense differs from the Turkish (4 rows):** matig (EN moderate, TR mediocre), prikken (EN pin prick, TR injection),
   geslaagd (EN successful, TR passed an exam), vertragen (EN slow down, TR delay). The owner picks one sense; the other
   language follows.
2. **The sense does not fit the category or is too narrow (3 rows):** beeldscherm "monitor" (any screen); levenslang "lifelong" in Government and law; hulpverlener "aid worker"
   (aid abroad) for the Dutch care/emergency helper.
3. **English above B1 or dated (3 rows):** bustle, utter, swindler.
4. **Hint problems (3 rows):** teek "tick (insect)" is factually wrong (the only `wrong` row); hals "neck (throat)" repeats
   A2 keel; chocolade "(formal word)" misdescribes a spelling variant (same row as the T-082 level finding).
5. **Not idiomatic (1 row):** inburgeren "to do civic integration".
