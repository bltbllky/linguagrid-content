## Mechanical style checks (all 504 rows)
- No noun starts with "the", "a" or "an"; every verb, and only the verbs, starts with "to" (117 verbs).
- British spelling throughout (organise, recognise, metre, centimetre, litre, programme, theatre, humour, lorry,
  motorway, washing line, fire brigade); no American spelling found.
- Capitals follow English rules: only the 12 months and 7 weekdays.
- `npm run validate -- --stage a1a2` reports no shared prompt. Every extras prompt whose bare text (without the bracket)
  equals another prompt in live A1/A2, B1 or the extras carries a bracket hint (61 pairs checked by hand).
- Every Dutch lemma has at most 12 letters; no offensive or sensitive word.

## Patterns
1. **Hint does not separate two synonyms (2 rows):** vrachtwagen "lorry (goods vehicle)" vs B1 truck "lorry",
   tijdschrift "magazine (periodical)" vs B1 magazine "magazine". Suggested: the register hint "(everyday word)", as
   getrouwd vs B1 gehuwd.
2. **Hint or bare word points to the wrong sense (4 rows):** pond (currency, in Shopping and money), rapport (written
   report, not the school report), testen "(try out)" in Health and body, verrassen "(unexpectedly)" (tautological).
3. **False friend (1 row):** merk "brand" is spelled like the Dutch extras word brand (fire), both A2. Same pattern as
   T-149 batterij "pil" and tak "dal".
4. **Follows a Turkish sense under review (1 row):** stiekem "sneaky" mirrors TR "sinsi" (T-149 finding).
