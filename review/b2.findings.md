## Patterns
- **Text (20 unsure rows):** most are calques or paraphrases, where an idiomatic Turkish word exists:
  - roeping, mantelzorg, huisman, halfzus, loverboy, alibi, dagdeel, afdracht, geoorloofd;
  - wrong register or sense: onwettig "kanunsuz" (lawless), tendentieus "yönlendirici", tekenend "belirleyici",
    voorbarig "aceleci", echtheid "orijinallik", notoir (the negative sense is lost), aantasten "zedelemek" (too narrow);
  - vakantiegeld is the employer's holiday allowance;
  - ongehoord is outrageous, not "unheard";
  - doorhakken lives in "de knoop doorhakken";
  - verhullen "örtbas etmek" is the sense of verdoezelen.
  Every suggestion was checked against every live, A1/A2, B1 and B2 prompt:
  - none equals another prompt or a Dutch lemma;
  - four draft suggestions were dropped because they are taken: devamsızlık (B1 afwezigheid), güvensizlik
    (B1 wantrouwen), damga (B1 stempel), kabul edilemez (B1 onacceptabel).
- **T-159 unsure prompts kept (no row):**
  - klankbord "fikir danışılan kişi" and sanering "yeniden yapılandırma" (financial sense, category money) are accurate;
  - opwaarderen "(bakiye) yüklemek": the OV-chipkaart top-up is the everyday sense;
  - decaan "dekan" is right for the education-research category;
  - dagbesteding "gündüz etkinlik programı" and ontlenen "(bir kaynaktan) almak" are clear with their hints.
- **Choices made to avoid collisions are kept:**
  - aanschaf "edinme": A2 aankoop has "satın alma";
  - verzuim "işe gelmeme": B1 afwezigheid has "devamsızlık";
  - onveiligheid "güvenlik sorunu";
  - stigma "damgalanma";
  - behendig "eli çabuk": B2 wendbaar has "çevik".
- **Near-synonyms of earlier words (3 removal rows):**
  - voltijds = A2 fulltime;
  - leefstijl = B1 levensstijl;
  - huizenmarkt = B1 woningmarkt.
  Same pattern as the B1 chocolade row. bewonderaar / A2 fan and vegetariër / B1 vegetarisch get no row: bewonderaar is
  an admirer, broader than a fan, and vegetariër is the person noun beside an adjective; both prompts carry a hint.
- **Pairs inside B2 are kept apart by their prompts, so no row:**
  - afwennen / afleren;
  - bevoordelen / voortrekken;
  - ontwijken / ontduiken;
  - stressvol / gestrest;
  - onjuist / onwaar;
  - verhullen / verdoezelen (one row on verhullen).
- **Level, too easy (block L1, 16 rows):** the 16 words on the T-159 list, all filed:
  - job words: schoonmaker, loodgieter, elektricien;
  - transparent compounds: kinderarts, oogarts, nachtleven, profielfoto, songtekst, zakenreis, ijskoud;
  - daily life: rijschool, bijles, spierpijn, stiefvader, stiefmoeder, piepklein.
  The A1/A2 extras and B1 are already filled, so the suggestion is "remove from B2". Removing all 16 leaves 1,559 words.
- **Level, too hard (block L2, 5 rows):** sommeren, aanhangig, beding, curatele, boedel are legal terms, rare outside
  legal texts. Other legal words (seponeren, heling, erfpacht, dagvaarding, borgtocht) are frequent in Dutch news and
  stay B2.
- **Category:** all 16 categories fit their words. sjouwen and mompelen are concrete verbs in "Soyut fiiller", but no B2
  category fits them better, so no row.
- **Sensitive words:** none offensive. These are B2 news, health and life vocabulary:
  - the T-158 list: homofobie, islamofobie, xenofobie, seksisme, mensenhandel, loverboy, drugsdealer, menstruatie,
    miskraam, schizofrenie, psychose, dement, verslaafde, overspel "zina", vreemdgaan;
  - added in T-159: homohuwelijk, atheïst, analfabeet, terreurdaad, ontlasting, wildplassen.
  The owner may want a look at overspel "zina". It is the exact legal equivalent, but the word has a religious tone in
  Turkish.
- **Lemma rule:** every lemma is a current lemma of 12 letters or fewer. There is no diminutive and no plural-only noun.
