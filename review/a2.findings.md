## Patterns
- **Articles:** all 399 A2 nouns have the right de/het. `het duin` and `het heimwee` also allow `de` (both forms are accepted);
  the stored article is the usual one in the Netherlands, so no change.
- **Verbs:** every verb prompt is an infinitive. Reflexive verbs (`schamen`, `vervelen`, `herinneren`) are stored without
  `zich`, which suits the grid.
- **Meaning:** `zenuwachtig = telaşlı` (flustered) misses "nervous"; `provincie = il` uses the Turkish unit; `neef` / `nicht`
  also mean nephew/niece. All owner decisions.
- **Style:** `dijk = set (bent)` puts a synonym in parentheses; `vorig`, `vrij`, `afspreken`, `feestdag` have a more natural
  everyday wording.
- **Categories:** five nouns (`beslissing`, `keus`, `oplossing`, `ontmoeting`, `bezoek`) sit in "Günlük fiiller", and `fout` in
  "Tanımlama". Optional moves; the pairing with their verbs may be on purpose.
- **Duplicates:** no two A2 words share a Turkish text. Shared head words carry a sense hint
  (`aandoen = açmak (cihaz)` / `openen = açmak`, `tong = dil (organ)` / `taal = dil (lisan)`, `maan = ay (gökyüzü)` / `maand = ay`).
- **Sensitive words:** none offensive. `wijn`, `drank`, `roken`, `liegen`, `overlijden`, `begrafenis`, `scheiden`,
  `zwanger` are everyday A2 vocabulary; no flag.
