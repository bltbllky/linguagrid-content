# A1 sentences: frames with no clean distractor set (proposals, T-381)

Date 2026-11-24. The independent distractor check flagged the rows below. Distractors were replaced by hand everywhere else. These frames stay weak because the blank can be excluded only by a world fact or the frame fits many words. Sentences and translations are NOT changed; the owner (or the PM) decides on a rewrite.

| id | now | problem | proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| noun-twee | Wij hebben twee ogen. | other numbers are excluded only by the fact "two eyes" (distractors now honderd, duizend, miljoen) | keep (fact is certain) or "Mijn broer en ik zijn twee jongens." |
| noun-vier | Een jaar heeft vier seizoenen. | same, fact only | keep or drop the word from the cloze set |
