## Patterns
- **Level, too easy (29 rows, one decision):** the 3k-10k frequency band (C2) let in concrete everyday words that are
  rare in news and Wikipedia text but are A1/A2 course words: home (koelkast, douche, wekker), forms (postcode,
  huisnummer, voornaam, achternaam), food (banaan, tomaat, peer, broodje, koekje), clothes (trui, hoed), animals (schaap,
  varken, eend, olifant), family (kleinkind), daily verbs (binnenkomen, thuisblijven, weggooien), and vriendelijk,
  vervelend, laptop, kapper, tandarts, dierentuin, bladzijde. The suggestion is to move them to the A1/A2 extras pool
  before T-146 (2026-11-09) runs; B1 would then have 1,171 words, still above the M3 target of 600 reviewed. A looser cut
  would add about 20 more (zeep, kaars, emmer, saus, menu, zoet, kers, laars, geit, beer, tijger, kikker, hoest, nagel,
  wang, sigaret, rugzak, inpakken, uitpakken, aanraken); those were left as B1.
- **Level, too hard (5 rows):** proefschrift, vennootschap, gerechtshof, witwassen, regelgeving are specialised (B2).
  Other formal words (stikstof, etmaal, staatsschuld, lijsttrekker) are frequent in Dutch news and stay B1.
- **Pairs with live A1/A2 words (C9):** chocolade = A2 chocola (spelling variant), friet = A2 patat (regional synonym),
  checken = A2 controleren, uitzetten = A2 uitdoen. aanzetten / A2 aandoen ("çalıştırmak (cihaz)" / "açmak (cihaz)") is
  the same kind of pair but its prompts are clear; no row.
- **Lemma rule:** chips is a plural (C1, C4), the only `wrong` row.
- **Text:** the T-080 "unsure" prompts were checked one by one. inhuren, afwezig, koopwoning, uitbetalen, gebit and
  statushouder get a suggestion; werkvloer `çalışma ortamı` matches the everyday "op de werkvloer" and is kept. Other
  suggestions: vergoeding, eiwit, opvoeding, toespraak, dialect, poos (a sense or register issue each).
- **Homographs** (vorst, exclusief, knap, nuchter, uitvallen, inhalen, beer) carry their B1 sense; no row.
- **Shared head words:** every B1 prompt that shares a head word with an A1/A2/B1 prompt has a sense hint
  (`bölüm (gazete)`, `taramak (belge)`, `donmak (bilgisayar)`, `iptal etmek` ×3, …); `npm run validate -- --stage b1`
  shows no shared prompt.
- **Categories:** all 21 categories fit their words; no row.
- **Playability:** every lemma has 4-12 letters and none is an A1/A2 lemma. **Sensitive words:** none offensive;
  racistisch, terrorist, schietpartij, witwassen, gokken, dronken, verslaving, schelden are B1 news and life vocabulary.
