LinguaGrid — Support

LinguaGrid is a free word-search game that teaches Dutch words at levels A1 and A2. You play in Turkish or English; it needs no account and shows no ads.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or ideas, email us at ugebdev@gmail.com. Mentioning your phone model and the app version (Settings → About & sources) helps us answer faster.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play without internet?

Yes. The words come with the app, so you can play offline from the first start.

Where is my progress stored?

Your level, word progress and settings are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us. There is no account, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I turn off the statistics?

In Settings, turn off "Share anonymous usage statistics". The statistics are anonymous and contain no personal data; see the privacy policy for details.

How do new words arrive?

When you are online, the app downloads new and corrected words in the background and applies them when you are not in the middle of a level. Your progress is kept. Without internet you keep playing with the words you have.
