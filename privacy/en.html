LinguaGrid — Privacy Policy

LinguaGrid is a free Dutch vocabulary game for Turkish speakers. This policy explains which data the app processes.

In short

No account, no ads, no tracking. We do not collect personal data that identifies you, and we never sell data.

Data on your device

Your chosen level, word progress and settings are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us. If your phone's backup (iCloud, Android backup) is on, the operating system may include them in your backup.

Anonymous usage statistics

To measure how many people use the app, we send anonymous events through Aptabase (servers in Germany/EU): first open (with the install week), weekly activity and use on day 30. If you answer the optional "Did the hint help?" question after a hint, your 👍 or 👎 is sent as well. They include the app version, operating system and release channel. No user, device or advertising identifiers are used; events cannot be linked to a person. You can turn the statistics off in Settings. Details: Aptabase privacy policy.

Content updates

The app downloads word updates and the minimum required version from GitHub Pages. GitHub may technically process your IP address in server logs: GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights (GDPR, KVKK)

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or correct. You remove the data on your device completely by deleting the app. Contact us with any question.
